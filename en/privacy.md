# Mortgage Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026.

This policy explains what data the Mortgage Tracker iOS app (named «Ипотечник» in Russian)
collects, why, where and for how long it is stored, and what you can do about it.
"We" means the developer of the app as listed on its App Store page.

## Summary

- The app sends us **the numbers you enter into a calculation and its results**: property
  price, down payment, rate, term, payment, overpayment and so on. This is financial data.
- **The app does not ask for or collect your income.**
- Calculation names and any text you type by hand are never sent anywhere.
- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location.
- We do not sell data, and the current version does not share it with partners.
- You cannot turn off analytics inside the app. How to stop the collection is described
  in "How to opt out".

## 1. What data we collect

### 1.1. Calculation inputs and results (financial data)

When you run a calculation or recalculate a scenario, the app sends:

- **calculation inputs:** property price, down payment as an amount and as a percentage,
  annual interest rate, loan term, payment type (annuity or declining), code of the
  selected currency;
- **your scenario values:** early repayment amount and mode, monthly rent and its annual
  growth, annual property value growth;
- **calculation results:** loan amount, monthly payment, total paid, interest paid to the
  bank, overpayment relative to the loan amount, resulting term.

**About income.** The app does not ask how much you earn and does not send any
information about your income, salary, savings or other loans. If this ever changes,
we will update this policy first.

These numbers are not linked to your name or Apple Account. They are linked to the app
installation identifier (section 1.3).

### 1.2. Usage data

- which screens you open and which actions you take: calculating, saving, deleting,
  exporting, expanding scenarios, changing the currency or theme;
- for calculation names, only the fact of renaming and the name length, never the text;
- purchase events: paywall views, the selected period, successful, cancelled or failed
  purchases with an error code, restoring purchases;
- ad impressions, if ads are enabled.

### 1.3. Identifiers

- **Firebase installation identifier** — a random identifier created when the app is
  installed and replaced when it is reinstalled. It contains no name, email or phone number.
- **Advertising identifier (IDFA)** — used for ads only, and only if you allow tracking
  in the iOS system prompt. App analytics does not use the IDFA.

### 1.4. Technical data

Device model, iOS and app version, interface language, approximate country based on the
IP address, session duration, information about technical failures.

### 1.5. What we do not collect

- names of saved calculations, a custom currency symbol, or any other text you type;
- your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos;
- precise location;
- payment details: subscriptions are processed by Apple, we never see your card number
  or Apple Account details.

### 1.6. What stays on your device only

Saved calculations, their names and app settings are stored on your device and are not
sent to us. Whether the free scenario recalculation has been used is stored in the iOS
secure storage (Keychain) on your device and survives reinstalling the app.

## 2. Why we collect data

1. **To run and improve the app:** to understand whether people reach a result, which
   calculations and scenarios they use, and where errors occur.
2. **To make monetization decisions:** to evaluate demand for the subscription and the
   effect of ads.
3. **To study which mortgage calculations people make:** which amounts, rates and terms
   are common. These statistics help develop the product, including possible services with
   partner banks in the future.
4. **To show ads**, if ads are enabled.

We do not use the data for automated decisions that have legal effects on you, and we do
not share it with banks.

## 3. Who receives the data

- **Google LLC** — processor for analytics and remote app configuration (Firebase
  Analytics, Firebase Remote Config) and for storage of exported analytics (Google
  BigQuery). Google processes the data on our behalf.
- **Google AdMob** — if ads are enabled in the app. AdMob may receive your approximate
  location based on the IP address, ad impression and click data and, only with your
  permission to track, the advertising identifier. Without permission, ads are
  non-personalized. With a Pro subscription no ads are shown and no requests are sent to AdMob.
- **Apple** — purchasing and renewing the subscription through the App Store. Apple
  processes payments under its own privacy policy.

Google servers may be located outside your country of residence.

**Partners.** The current version does not share data with banks, brokers or any other
third parties. If such sharing is introduced, we will ask for your separate consent in the
app and update this policy. Data collected before such consent will not be shared with
partners.

We do not sell data. We may disclose it if required by law or by a binding request from
a public authority.

## 4. How long data is kept

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Analytics events in Firebase Analytics linked to the installation identifier | 14 months from collection |
| Analytics events exported to Google BigQuery | 3 years from collection, then deleted |
| Aggregated statistics not linked to an identifier | No time limit |
| Ad data in Google AdMob | Under Google's rules |
| Saved calculations and settings on the device | Until you delete them or delete the app |
| Free recalculation flag in Keychain | Until the device is reset |

## 5. How to opt out

**The app has no switch that turns off analytics** and no separate consent screen.
By using the app, you agree to the data collection described in this policy. If you do
not agree, please do not use the app.

What you can do:

- **Stop collection entirely** — delete the app. Once deleted, no more data is sent.
  A reinstall creates a new installation identifier, and new data is not linked to the old.
- **Disallow tracking for ads** — in iOS settings: Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Tracking, or Settings → Mortgage Tracker → Allow Tracking. Ads remain but become
  non-personalized.
- **Delete saved calculations** — in the app, by swiping a calculation in the history.
  Deleting the app deletes them too.
- **Cancel the subscription** — in your Apple Account settings: Settings → your name →
  Subscriptions. Cancelling does not affect analytics.

A link to this policy is always available in the app: Settings → Privacy Policy and
Settings → About.

**Data deletion requests.** We do not know who you are: the collected data is linked only
to a random installation identifier, so we cannot find a specific person's data by name or
email. You can send a request using the contacts below, and we will tell you what can be
done in your case.

## 6. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly
collect data from children.

## 7. Security

Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the developer has access
to analytics and exported data.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. A new version is published at the same address with a new
date. We will notify you in the app about material changes, such as sharing data with
partners, before they take effect.
